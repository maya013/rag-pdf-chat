The university accepts admission applications for the Fall semester until September 30. Applications received after the deadline will not be considered.

The Bachelor of Computer Science is a four-year degree consisting of eight semesters. Students study programming, artificial intelligence, databases, and software engineering.

Hostel accommodation is available for students coming from other cities. Students can apply for accommodation after receiving their admission offer.

Tuition fees can be deposited through the university bank account. Students must upload the payment receipt through the student portal.

Applicants must provide their previous academic transcripts, identification documents, recent photographs, and any required equivalence certificates.
